Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our staging workflow, delivery timelines, pricing tiers, and real estate ROI across the UAE.

In the UAE market (especially on Bayut and Property Finder), over 90% of buyers filter properties based on initial photo quality and spatial layout. Staged properties create an immediate emotional connection, allowing buyers to visualize living in the space rather than focusing on empty rooms or layout flaws. This leads to significantly higher viewing requests and faster closing offers.

We work with both! Ar. Alina Nazish collaborates directly with individual property owners who are selling or leasing independently, as well as licensed real estate agents, brokers, and agency teams looking to elevate their listing presentation, increase portal click-through rates, and close sales faster across Dubai and the UAE.

The Essential tier is designed to give your property listing instant high-converting appeal. It includes a professional architectural photo shoot, a cinematic property video walkthrough, and a expertly crafted ad draft description optimized for high search positioning on real estate portals.

The Most Popular tier combines all photography, video, and ad copy deliverables from the Essential package with a custom 2D/3D architectural layout design. Having a clear 2D/3D layout enables buyers and brokers to understand room dimensions and optimal furniture arrangement immediately, eliminating spatial uncertainty.

For older properties, distressed units, or high-end luxury villas that require basic structural updates or aesthetic overhauls, Ar. Alina Nazish provides turnkey interior design, material selection, contractor management, and physical furniture staging. Pricing is provided via custom quote following an initial site audit.

We service properties across all UAE emirates: Dubai (Downtown, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Business Bay, Hills Estate), Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain.

On-site shoots are typically scheduled within 24 to 48 hours of booking. All final edited photography, video assets, and ad descriptions are delivered within 48 hours post-shoot.
