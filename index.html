ARCHITECTURAL PHILOSOPHY

Enduring Architecture Rooted in Spatial Proportion

We believe exceptional architecture emerges from the dialog between human scale, natural light, and authentic materiality. Ar. Alina Nazish guides each commission from initial schematic inquiry to realized structural reality.

01

Context & Schematic Discovery

Analysis of orientation, natural daylight paths, volumetric massing, and functional circulation to establish the project's spatial foundation.

02

Spatial & Architectural Layout

Rigorous 2D/3D spatial planning, structural alignment, sightline framing, and volume choreography for refined living.

03

Materiality & Detailing

Selection of noble materials—natural stone, fine woods, tactile metals—paired with comprehensive technical construction drawings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key insights into our architectural design process, project timelines, municipal approvals, and collaborative methodology.

naz by nazish specializes in bespoke architectural design for private residences and villas, full interior architecture and spatial reconfiguration for luxury penthouses and apartments, and concept-to-completion design for boutique commercial & hospitality spaces (specialty cafés, bakeries, and retail). We also provide detailed construction documentation and site design supervision across the UAE.

We collaborate with private homeowners commissioning ground-up residences or extensive remodels, commercial entrepreneurs creating distinctive hospitality concepts, and boutique developers seeking distinctive, high-value spatial design throughout Dubai and the UAE.

Our process consists of 4 distinct phases: 1) Contextual Briefing & Site Discovery, 2) Schematic Architecture & 2D/3D Spatial Layouts, 3) Material Specification & Detailed Technical Construction Blueprints, and 4) Periodic Site Supervision & Artisan Coordination during execution to ensure design integrity.

Yes. We produce complete architectural CAD/BIM drawing packages including detailed floor plans, internal elevations, joinery and millwork shop drawings, and MEP/lighting coordination sheets required for contractors and authority approval consultants.

We regularly collaborate with client-appointed general contractors, fit-out specialists, and MEP engineers. Additionally, through our extensive UAE practice, we can introduce trusted specialized artisans, stone fabricators, and custom millwork joineries to execute bespoke details flawlessly.

We accept commissions across all seven UAE Emirates: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain, as well as select international design advisory commissions across the GCC.
